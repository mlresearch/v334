abstract: Graph neural network architectures based on the graph Laplacian approximate
  the Laplace-Beltrami operator, thus limiting their application to isotropic operators.
  As a nonlinear alternative to the Laplace-Beltrami operator, we consider estimates
  of the Finsler Laplacian on point clouds sampled from a manifold. We prove that
  these discrete estimates converge to the true operator on the manifold as the number
  of point samples grows. Moreover, we show that this operator can be expressed as
  a graph neural network layer, which we use to define a family of Finslerian graph
  neural networks constrained to express Finsler geometry. We show that Finslerian
  graph neural networks recover the geometry underlying nonlinear diffusion equations
  in practice.
