abstract: 'This paper describes the 2026 edition of the \textit{Topological Deep Learning Challenge: Bridging the Gap}, hosted at the second Topology, Algebra, and Geometry in Data Science (TAG-DS) Conference. Where previous editions grew the modelling and data ecosystem of Topological Deep Learning (TDL), this edition set out to place Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and Topological Neural Networks (TNNs) side by side under a single evaluation pipeline. Participants implemented recent state-of-the-art architectures in \texttt{TopoBench}, with Track 1 covering GNNs and Track 2 covering TNNs. They evaluated these implementations on shared synthetic benchmarks generated with \texttt{GraphUniverse}, which independently controls homophily, average degree and degree-distribution heaviness. The challenge yielded 68 qualifying submissions from 34 teams, spanning the graph, simplicial, cellular, hypergraph and combinatorial domains. For the first time, submissions were also tracked on a public leaderboard, which we summarize and analyze here. Our analysis finds that the differences between the submitted tracks are strongly task-dependent: the mean accuracy gap is modest on community detection, while on triangle counting the Track 1 mean error is 1.85 times the Track 2 mean. Several structurally informed GNNs also outperform the Track 2 mean on triangle counting.'
